## POI-001 Dinh Độc Lập

English name: Independence Palace.

### English description

Independence Palace was designed by Vietnamese architect Ngo Viet Thu and inaugurated in 1966. Its modern architecture incorporates traditional East Asian ideas and decorative forms. Once the residence and workplace of the president of South Vietnam, the palace preserves reception halls, meeting rooms, and a basement command centre. It is closely associated with the events of 30 April 1975.

Sources: [Palace architecture, official historical-site website](https://dinhdoclap.gov.vn/en/independence-palace-architecture/); [Historical significance, Department of Cultural Heritage](https://dsvh.gov.vn/di-tich-lich-su-dinh-doc-lap-2937).

## POI-002 Nhà thờ Đức Bà Sài Gòn

English name: Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon.

### English description

Built between 1877 and 1880, Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon is a landmark of the city's French colonial period. Architect J. Bourard combined Romanesque and Gothic influences in its design. The cathedral's exposed brick exterior and religious architecture reflect that heritage. It became the cathedral of the Archdiocese of Saigon in 1960 and received minor basilica status in 1962.

Source: [Cathedral history, official cathedral website](https://www.chinhtoasaigon.org/gioi-thieu).

Research note: The cathedral's [official homepage](https://www.chinhtoasaigon.org/) states that it is closed to tourists during restoration while remaining open for daily Mass. This notice was checked on the research date. It is separate from the historical description and must be rechecked before publishing visitor-access guidance.

## POI-003 Bưu Điện Trung Tâm Sài Gòn

English name: Saigon Central Post Office.

### English description

Near Notre-Dame Cathedral, Saigon Central Post Office offers a glimpse of the city's historic communications architecture. Its spacious entrance hall contains wooden telephone booths, old maps, and a prominent portrait of Ho Chi Minh. The interior makes the building a distinctive stop among the historic landmarks of central Saigon.

Source: [Saigon Central Post Office section, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/7-must-see-attractions-hcmc).

Research note: This draft does not attribute the building's design to Gustave Eiffel; that attribution is disputed in the source. Published admission and opening-hour information in the same general tourism article is not used in this draft.

## POI-004 Nhà hát Thành phố

English name: Ho Chi Minh City Opera House.

### English description

Ho Chi Minh City Opera House stands on Lam Son Square. Built between 1898 and 1900, it features an ornate facade influenced by French theatre architecture. The building served other public functions during the twentieth century before returning to performing arts. Today it hosts music and theatre, connecting the city's architectural heritage with its cultural life.

Source: [Ho Chi Minh City theatre record, national tourism database](https://csdl.vietnamtourism.gov.vn/dest/?item=381).

## POI-005 Bến Nhà Rồng

English name: Nha Rong Wharf.

### English description

Nha Rong Wharf is associated with a turning point in the life of Ho Chi Minh. On 5 June 1911, the young Nguyen Tat Thanh departed from here to begin his journey overseas. The site is now associated with the Ho Chi Minh Museum's city branch, allowing visitors to explore the story of his life and the historical significance of that departure.

Sources: [The historic journey, Ho Chi Minh Museum](https://baotanghochiminh.vn/chuyen-di-lich-su.htm); [Nha Rong Wharf and the museum branch, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://vietnamtourism.gov.vn/post/50826).

## POI-006 Bảo tàng Chứng tích Chiến tranh

English name: War Remnants Museum.

### English description

The War Remnants Museum documents the human consequences of war in Vietnam through photographs, documents, and historical objects. Its predecessor opened in September 1975. The collections explain the destruction caused by conflict and the experiences of those affected. The museum also presents a message of peace and friendship between peoples.

Source: [War Remnants Museum profile, Department of Cultural Heritage museum directory](https://baotang.dsvh.gov.vn/vi-VN/Detail/Index/118).

## POI-007 Bảo tàng Lịch sử Việt Nam

English name: Ho Chi Minh City History Museum.

### English description

Established in 1929, the Ho Chi Minh City History Museum introduces Vietnam's history from prehistoric times to 1945. Its collections also explore the cultures of southern Vietnam and other parts of Asia. The museum building combines Eastern and Western architectural influences, making the setting itself part of the historical experience.

Source: [Museum introduction and history, official museum website](https://www.baotanglichsutphcm.com.vn/gioi-thieu).

Research note: The user's label is retained as the Vietnamese heading. The researched institution identifies itself as Bảo tàng Lịch sử Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh. This record refers to that museum, not the Vietnam National Museum of History in Hanoi or the separate Ho Chi Minh City Museum.

## POI-008 Bảo tàng Mỹ thuật TP.HCM

English name: Ho Chi Minh City Fine Arts Museum.

### English description

The Ho Chi Minh City Fine Arts Museum presents Vietnamese art across three exhibition buildings. Its displays include modern works, themed collections, and art from earlier periods. Paintings and objects made from stone, ceramics, bronze, and wood offer different perspectives on artistic expression. The museum provides a setting for exploring the visual heritage of southern Vietnam and the country as a whole.

Source: [Fine Arts Museum profile, Department of Cultural Heritage museum directory](https://baotang.dsvh.gov.vn/vi-VN/Detail/Index/122).

Research note: Public tourism articles give conflicting years for the museum's first public opening. No opening year is included in this description.

## POI-009 Tòa nhà Landmark 81

English name: Landmark 81.

### English description

Landmark 81 is an eighty-one-storey tower whose clustered form draws inspiration from a bundle of bamboo, a Vietnamese symbol of strength and unity. The building combines residences, shopping, hospitality, and observation spaces. From its upper observation levels, visitors can view the surrounding city from above, experiencing a different perspective on modern Ho Chi Minh City.

Source: [Landmark 81 building and observation areas, Vinpearl](https://vinpearl.com/en/landmark-81-vietnam-discover-a-resort-paradise-in-the-clouds-in-saigon).

Research note: Rankings, prices, opening times, and specific entertainment offerings are omitted because they can change. No floor-area figure is used because the source gives inconsistent figures.

## POI-010 Tòa nhà Bitexco

English name: Bitexco Financial Tower.

### English description

Bitexco Financial Tower is a sixty-eight-storey landmark designed by architect Carlos Zapata. Its form was inspired by a lotus bud, linking a modern skyscraper with a familiar Vietnamese cultural symbol. The tower's Saigon Skydeck on the forty-ninth floor offers a panoramic view of Ho Chi Minh City and its changing urban landscape.

Sources: [Tower project, developer Bitexco](https://bitexco.com.vn/en/project/bitexco-financial-tower/); [Saigon Skydeck, official tower website](https://bitexcofinancialtower.com/).

Research note: The two official pages were available as indexed search extracts; direct page retrieval timed out during research. Floor count, architect, and lotus inspiration are supported by the developer's extract; the Skydeck floor is supported by the tower website's extract. Recheck the full pages before final editorial sign-off.

## POI-011 Thảo Cầm Viên Sài Gòn

English name: Saigon Zoo and Botanical Gardens.

### English description

Founded in 1864, Saigon Zoo and Botanical Gardens brings together animal collections, mature trees, and a long history of botanical study. It has developed from an early plant nursery and animal garden into a place for conservation, wildlife rescue, and public education. Its green spaces also preserve generations of memories connected with the growth of Saigon.

Source: [Formation and development, official Saigon Zoo website](https://www.saigonzoo.vn/thao-cam-vien-sai-gon-qua-trinh-hinh-thanh-va-phat-trien-57.html).

## POI-012 Phố đi bộ Nguyễn Huệ

English name: Nguyen Hue Walking Street.

### English description

Nguyen Hue Walking Street is a broad public promenade in central Ho Chi Minh City. Stretching from the City Hall area towards Bach Dang Wharf, it provides space to walk and observe the city's urban life. Historic buildings, modern towers, shops, and restaurants frame the street, which becomes especially lively around sunset.

Source: [Nguyen Hue Street and City Hall section, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/7-must-see-attractions-hcmc).

## POI-013 Phố Tây Bùi Viện

English name: Bui Vien Walking Street.

### English description

Bui Vien is known for its lively nightlife and international backpacker atmosphere. Its restaurants and street-food stalls bring together Vietnamese dishes and food from other countries. Music, bars, and street performances shape the evening experience. The street became an official walking street in 2017 and remains associated with the city's tourism and entertainment scene.

Source: [Bui Vien section, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/ho-chi-minh-city-cuisine-street-eats-fine-dining).

Research note: The description does not imply that vehicles are prohibited at all times; the current pedestrian schedule requires a separate check before providing navigation instructions.

## POI-014 Địa đạo Củ Chi

English name: Cu Chi Tunnels at Ben Duoc.

Confirmed visitor destination: Bến Dược, selected by the user on 22 October 2026 in DEC-009.

### English description

Ben Duoc is part of the Cu Chi Tunnels historical site. The tunnel network developed during resistance to French colonial rule and expanded during the Vietnam War. Its connected passages supported shelter, communication, storage, medical care, and everyday activity underground. The site offers insight into the ingenuity and difficult living conditions of those who used the tunnels.

Source: [Cu Chi Tunnels historical site, Department of Cultural Heritage](https://dsvh.gov.vn/di-tich-lich-su-dia-dao-cu-chi-1490).

Research note: The destination choice is resolved by DEC-009: use Ben Duoc for this POI. Coordinates for the appropriate visitor entrance still require verification. The description draws on the historical context of the wider tunnel network; it does not claim that every facility described by the source is accessible at Ben Duoc today.

## POI-015 Chùa Bà Thiên Hậu

English name: Thien Hau Temple in Cholon.

### English description

Thien Hau Temple is a religious and cultural landmark of Cholon, closely connected with the city's Chinese community. It is dedicated to the goddess Thien Hau. Decorative ceramic scenes illustrate stories from Chinese tradition. The temple introduces visitors to a living religious heritage within the wider cultural landscape of Ho Chi Minh City.

Sources: [Thien Hau Temple and Chinese-community heritage, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism thematic portal](https://dantoc.vietnamtourism.gov.vn/chua-ba-thien-hau-khong-gian-tin-nguong-va-van-hoa-nguoi-hoa-giua-long-sai-gon/); [Cholon and Thien Hau Temple, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism](https://vietnamtourism.gov.vn/en/post/18968).

Research note: These sources were read through indexed search extracts; direct retrieval failed or returned no article body during this session. The first extract supports the community connection; the second supports dedication to Thien Hau and the ceramic scenes. Recheck the full articles before final editorial sign-off. This record is for the temple in Cholon, not a different Thien Hau temple elsewhere in the current city boundary.
