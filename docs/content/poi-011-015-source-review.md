## Method and evidence boundaries

Used the available web search tool to discover pages, then opened original institutional pages and read their article text. The search tool does not identify its engine as Google; **Google-specific searching was not verified**. No claim of using Google or a native browser is made. Search snippets alone are not accepted as the final basis for narration. Direct article reads are recorded below; this is editorial research, not an on-site survey or a test of current access. The access date is the research date, not the publication date or a guarantee that a page is current.

Scripts paraphrase selected facts in original English and add clearly identifiable invitations to observe or reflect. Those invitations are editorial framing, not historical evidence. Prices, transport rules, live events, business inventories, coordinates and current visitor entrances are excluded. No new administrative boundary/address claim is made from old district labels in articles.

## Excluded, conflicting or inaccessible material

- **Zoo:** Z1 gives different corporate conversion years in its narrative and milestone list (2015/2010). Neither enters narration. Current animal/plant counts, species inventories and oldest-zoo ranking are excluded; no numerical conservation outcome is claimed.
- **Nguyen Hue:** N1 contains historical district labels, ticket figures and a categorical City Hall access statement. None establishes present administrative location, pricing or entry rights; excluded. No specific festival or pedestrian schedule supplied.
- **Bui Vien:** B1's broad vehicle-ban language is not treated as a 24-hour rule. [2017 opening report](https://vietnamtourism.gov.vn/en/post/12068) appeared in search with historical time restrictions, but direct open returned only a page shell. It is not accepted as full-text verification and is not used to supply a present schedule. No other source confirms current restrictions in this research.
- **Cu Chi:** C1 and N1 give differing network-length figures; no tunnel-length statistic enters narration. Ben Duoc entrance coordinates, accessible sections and operating status remain outside this research. Domain-restricted discovery for `diadaocuchi.com.vn` did not yield a usable operator article; no operator-page verification claimed. C1 describes restored features, but this work does not classify each present display as original/restored on site.
- **Temple:** [Earlier national portal URL](https://vietnamtourism.gov.vn/en/post/18968) returned only a page shell on direct open, so it is not relied on. T1/T2 differ in precision/community emphasis around foundation history; the script omits exact foundation date and dialect-group origin detail beyond T2's Cantonese association.
- **Wrong-place risk:** [Laydy Thien Hau Pagoda Festival](https://visithcmc.vn/listing/highlight-event/laydy-thien-hau-pagoda-festival/) mixes a Cholon-looking heading/address with a Thu Dau Mot/Binh Duong festival narrative. Only a search-rendered result was inspected; the page was not directly opened. Excluded entirely as evidence for POI015. Do not import its first-lunar-month festival into the Cholon script.
