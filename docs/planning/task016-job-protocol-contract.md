## Claim/renew/reclaim protocol

POST /internal/jobs/claim strict {workerId,capabilities:[unique language codes,max16]}; authenticated configured local caller; workerId must match header/config. Body only requests intersection of server-owned local_worker audio registry capabilities. Empty/unsupported intersection422, never provider fallback. Factory internal adapter claims only its server_adapter translation/audio scope. Claim queued eligible jobs sorted createdAt then ID. In one Content transaction check snapshot/current draft/nondeleted, mark stale jobs failed without touching localized output, or acquire one job with random attemptId,leaseOwner and leaseExpiresAt=serverNow+300000.200 {data:{job:{id,taskType,languageCode,inputVersion,inputSnapshot,attemptId,leaseExpiresAt,retryCount}},requestId}; none200 data:{job:null}. Snapshot is returned only to its authorized scoped processor.

Running job expired at serverNow (<=) may be reclaimed; increment reclaimCount, replace attemptId/owner/lease, preserving original job snapshot and requester/session. After3reclaims, next expiry transitions failed LEASE_RECLAIM_EXHAUSTED and requires Staff retry. No background wake/ping/scheduler; bounded request-driven reconciliation while processors actively claim, at most32candidate jobs per claim. No eligible job within that bound returns no work; not a global fairness guarantee. Failed stale/reclaim transitions append job.complete failed outbox with original actor/session; fixed result errorCode PROCESSING_FAILED. No physical provider job rollback promise.

POST /internal/jobs/:id/renew strict {attemptId}; authenticated owner, running matching attempt and unexpired lease/current snapshot/nondeleted draft. Set expiry=serverNow+300000. Expired/lost attempt409 STALE_JOB_ATTEMPT, no resurrection. No increment editVersion for lease-only metadata. Transaction uses same job document conditional update; content mutations are serialized on actual parent/version writes when accepting output. Renew/reclaim do not grant publish permission.
